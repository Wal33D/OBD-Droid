# Code of Conduct

OBD-Droid is a Somnus Labs Inc. project. This code applies to everyone who takes part in its spaces: GitHub
issues, Discussions and pull requests, and any community channel Somnus Labs runs for OBD-Droid (for example a
Discord server).

## Our pledge

We want taking part to be welcoming for everyone, whatever their background or experience, from someone
plugging in their first adapter to a professional technician.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Answering beginner questions patiently; everyone started somewhere.
- Accepting feedback gracefully and giving it kindly.
- Being honest about what you tested and what you didn't.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Posting someone else's private information without permission, **including their VIN, licence plate,
  location or vehicle logs**.
- Advice meant to make a vehicle unsafe or illegal to drive, such as disabling safety systems or defeating
  emissions controls.
- Spam, or promoting products or services without saying you're connected to them.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Somnus Labs maintainers enforce this code. They may edit or remove posts, comments, commits and other
contributions that break it, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
harmful. They will explain moderation decisions where that is safe to do.

Report a problem privately to **[conduct contact email]**. Reports are kept confidential. If the post contains
someone's private information, say so and it will be removed first.
